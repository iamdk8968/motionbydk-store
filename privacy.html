Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Motion by DK (operated by Dhananjay Kumar, independent software and digital asset developer). We build professional workflow tools for Adobe After Effects and curate high-resolution video editing asset libraries.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit our website (www.motionbydk.in) or purchase our products.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver and maintain your digital products:

  • Purchase Information: Name, email address, transaction reference / UTR number, and country.
  • License Activation Data: Extension license key, computer hardware hash (for 2-machine activation verification inside After Effects), and software version.
  • Free Starter Pack Subscribers: Name and email address submitted voluntarily to receive free download links.
  • Communication Data: Inquiries sent to our support desk (support@motionbydk.in).

3. How We Process Payments

We do not collect or store credit card, debit card, or bank account numbers on our servers. All transactions are securely routed through certified payment partners:

  • Gumroad, Inc.: For international card and PayPal transactions. Gumroad is PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant.
  • SuperProfile & UPI: For Indian domestic transactions via UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and domestic NetBanking.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Issuing and verifying your 2-PC AE Workflow Pro license keys.
  • Delivering download links for the 50GB Editing Vault and Free Starter Kit via Google Drive.
  • Sending product update announcements and critical bug fixes.
  • Providing technical customer support and installation assistance.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email address or data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Rights

We retain transaction records to honor your lifetime license and free update rights. You have the right at any time to request access to your stored data, license status, or email removal by contacting us at support@motionbydk.in.
