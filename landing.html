Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Adobe After Effects versions are supported? +

MotionByDk AE Workflow Pro supports Adobe After Effects CC 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and CC 2026+. It works on both macOS (Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4 & Intel) and Windows 10/11.

How do I install the extension? +

We include automatic 1-click installer scripts. On Windows, double-click install_windows.bat. On Mac, double-click install_mac.sh. Then open After Effects > Window > Extensions > MotionByDk AEworkflow.

How does license activation work? +

Immediately after your Gumroad purchase, you will receive a unique 35-character License Key on your receipt and email. Simply open the extension in After Effects, paste your key, and click "Activate License"!

Do I have to pay for future updates? +

No! All future updates are 100% free for existing buyers. The plugin includes an in-app update notification banner that notifies you directly inside After Effects whenever a new release is available.
