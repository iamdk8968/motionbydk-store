Our 7-Day Technical Compatibility Guarantee

Because MotionByDk products are digital software extensions accompanied by permanent commercial license keys, our refund policy focuses on genuine technical compatibility. If our extension cannot be installed or run on your supported operating system and Adobe After Effects CC version after you contact developer support, you are entitled to a 100% full refund.

Eligibility Requirements

  • 1. Time Window: Your refund request must be submitted within 7 calendar days from the date of your initial purchase.
  • 2. Supported Environment: Your system meets our published specifications (Adobe After Effects CC 2019 through CC 2026+ on Windows 10/11 64-bit or macOS 10.15+ Intel & Apple Silicon).
  • 3. Support Verification: You reached out to our developer support via email or WhatsApp and provided reasonable error logs or screenshots so we had the opportunity to troubleshoot the installation.

Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are not granted under the following circumstances:

  • ✕ "Change of mind" after the license key has already been issued, activated on 2 machines, and the software is functioning properly without defect.
  • ✕ Purchasing for an explicitly unsupported host application (e.g. attempting to run an After Effects CEP plugin in Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve).
  • ✕ Refusal to follow the standard Adobe CEP troubleshooting steps (such as enabling PlayerDebugMode or verifying extension folder path) before claiming non-compatibility.
  • ✕ Requests made after the 7-day warranty window has expired.
