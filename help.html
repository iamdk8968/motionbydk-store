OFFICIAL INSTALLATION GUIDE

Installing MotionByDk AE Workflow Pro

Works seamlessly on After Effects CC 2019 through CC 2026+ on both macOS & Windows.

🪟 Option A: Windows 10 / 11 Installation

Method 1 (Automated 1-Click Batch):

  1. Unzip the downloaded package.
  2. Right-click install_windows.bat and select Run as administrator.
  3. Restart Adobe After Effects.

Method 2 (Manual Copy to CEP folder):

Copy the extension folder into your Windows CEP directory:

C:\Program Files (x86)\Common Files\Adobe\CEP\extensions\
(Or user-specific directory: %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\)
🍎 Option B: macOS (Apple Silicon M1-M4 & Intel)

Method 1 (Automated Terminal Script):

  1. Unzip the downloaded package.
  2. Open Terminal, navigate to the folder, and run: chmod +x install_mac.sh && ./install_mac.sh
  3. Restart Adobe After Effects.

Method 2 (Manual Copy in Finder):

In Finder, press Cmd + Shift + G (Go to Folder) and paste:

~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/
Copy the extension folder directly into this directory, then restart After Effects.
🎉 Opening the Panel in After Effects:

After installing and restarting After Effects, open the top menu bar:

Window → Extensions → MotionByDk AE Workflow Pro

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

The extension does not appear under Window → Extensions. What should I do?

This is usually because Adobe CEP requires Developer Mode enabled to load unsigned or custom CEP panels.

For macOS: Open Terminal and execute this command:

defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.10 PlayerDebugMode 1 && defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.11 PlayerDebugMode 1 && defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.12 PlayerDebugMode 1

For Windows: Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:

reg add HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Adobe\CSXS.10 /v PlayerDebugMode /t REG_SZ /d 1 /f

Then restart After Effects and check Window → Extensions.

How many computers can I activate with my license key?

Each commercial license permits activation on up to 2 computers simultaneously (e.g. desktop workstation and laptop). You do not need to buy separate licenses for Windows and Mac.

If you get a new computer or reinstall your OS, you can reset your active machine slots instantly through our Self-Service License Portal using OTP verification to your purchase email.
